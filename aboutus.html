About Us!

Animals in need can find a safe sanctuary at Green Scared Heart Pet Sanctuary. We provide abused or abandoned animals with a second opportunity in a caring, tranquil setting by rescuing, caring for, and protecting them. Because every life matters, every animal here is handled with kindness, decency, and care. The Rescue was created in the 90s to support small Animals in need during trying times and Large weather events, but present day is open to cat future pets!
